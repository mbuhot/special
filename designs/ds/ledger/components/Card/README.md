A bordered surface that groups related content, with an optional title and footer.

Provide: a title (`ds-card__title`) and the content. `ds-card--row` lays children in a row aligned to their bottom edge, for inline forms.

- Use a card per topic on a page; stack cards with `ds-stack` and place them side by side with `ds-grid`.
- Put the card action in `ds-card__footer`.
