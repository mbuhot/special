# Adversarial review after iteration 1 (commit a6afddd)

Verdict: FAIL. Screens are scaffolds showing one or two fields per record; the invoice editor has data-integrity bugs.

## User experience gaps (B = blocks a pass)

1. B Invoice editor: no customer shown or chosen; no issue date, due date, per-line total, remove line, tax, currency, notes, PDF or email.
2. B Contact page: only the activity form and list. No email, phone, company, deals, or author per activity. Kinds lowercase, ISO dates.
3. B Deal page: no amount, company, contact, close date, owner, stage controls; stage shown raw.
4. B Company page: tables without headings; no domain, industry, deals, activity; contacts and invoices not links; raw status.
5. B Login is email only; no logout; `/` serves the stock Phoenix page.
6. Pipeline board: no value or count per column, unformatted amounts, no owner or close date.
7. Dashboard: stage counts not links; overdue card has no link and no task list page exists; unpaid counts drafts; no money figures.
8. Lists: Email, Domain, Industry columns blank in every recording; no sorting, pagination, owner or last-activity column.
9. Editors: Save stays on the editor; no Cancel; deal editor lists every contact.
10. Invoice numbering: INV-1 vs INV-001.
11. No delete or archive anywhere.
12. Identity block shows a role label in place of a name; no user management.

## Code quality

1. B invoice_live/edit.ex:21-25,135-144: every keystroke updates every line; update result discarded; line items editable after draft; row id unchecked.
2. B invoice_live/index.ex:32-42: New invoice attaches to the oldest company.
3. B billing.ex:34-42: next_invoice_number loads every invoice unauthorized and is racy.
4. B login_live.ex, session_controller.ex:8: authentication is knowing an email.
5. activity.ex:31 accepts user_id (assign_user.ex uses change_new_attribute); deal.ex:35 accepts owner_id; task.ex:42 accepts assignee_id. A client can act as another user.
6. deal.ex:104: no check that the contact belongs to the deal's company.
7. Editors never check authorization on mount.
8. Overdue logic duplicated (pipeline.ex:40-44, deal_live/show.ex:63-66); open-stage list duplicated (pipeline.ex:8, board.ex:9).
9. Ash calls in the web layer: board.ex:27, company_live/show.ex:15, contact_live/edit.ex:31 (authorize?: false); per-deal Ash.can? in a loop (board.ex:58).
10. pipeline.ex:32-37 three counts where one grouped aggregate would do; crm.ex:28 hand-written query.
11. Search patterns do not escape % and \_; String.to_integer on params in invoice_live/edit.ex:102.
12. layouts.ex boilerplate comments; dates formatted three ways.

## Spec quality

1. Invoice editor surface omits Description, Add line, company and date fields; Login has no surface.
2. Model has no types, optionality or enums (D4).
3. "Belongs to User" never names the role (owner, assignee, author) or who may set it.
4. Derivations unspecified: Invoice.total, overdue, unpaid, invoice number format, recent-activity limit (D7).
5. "Creating an invoice from the list" never chooses a customer.
6. No invariant freezes line items outside draft or ties a deal's contact to its company (D9).
7. Detail surfaces declare only what scenarios assert; no property fields.
8. Scenarios assert raw enum strings ("lead 2").

## Recommended next change

Record pages for contact, company and deal: properties panel with every model field, associations as links, stage and status controls, timeline with author and formatted dates. Then the invoice editor: customer selection, per-line totals, read-only outside draft, persisted-or-error edits.
